# **App Name**: PropertyIQ

## Core Features:

- ROI Calculator: Calculate potential return on investment for real estate properties based on user inputs.
- Property Listings & Details: Browse and view detailed information for available investment properties.
- AI-Powered Investment Analysis Tool: Provides data-driven insights and identifies key investment metrics for specific properties, leveraging AI to analyze complex data sets and highlight opportunities or risks.
- Mortgage Simulation: Simulate various mortgage scenarios to understand financial implications and payment structures.
- AI-Generated Market Trend Reports: Access concise, AI-generated reports summarizing current real estate market trends and forecasts, identifying impactful patterns and shifts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep Ocean Blue (#245B7D) to convey professionalism and reliability, a rich, grounded tone for interactive elements and accents.
- Background color: Muted Horizon Grey (#F0F4F6), a very light blue-grey that offers a clean, airy canvas without being stark white.
- Accent color: Vibrant Teal (#4AC2AE), an energetic, growth-oriented hue providing contrast for key actions and highlighted data, reminiscent of positive trends.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), chosen for its contemporary, techy, and precise feel, aligning with the app's analytical nature.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), selected for its excellent readability and neutral aesthetic, ideal for displaying data, reports, and longer textual content.
- Utilize a clean, minimalist icon set with a professional tone, focusing on clarity and immediate recognition for financial and real estate concepts.
- Implement a structured and intuitive dashboard layout with clear data visualization, emphasizing readability and efficient access to information.
